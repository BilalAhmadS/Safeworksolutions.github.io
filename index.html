About Us

About Safework Solutions & Consultants

SSC – Safework Solutions & Consultants (SMC) Pvt. Ltd. is a professional Health, Safety and Quality training institute based in Gujar Khan, Pakistan. We offer internationally recognised certifications — including NEBOSH, IOSH, OTHM, NVQ, QUALIFI, PMP, Lean Six Sigma and ISO CQI IRCA — delivered by highly experienced trainers with deep industry knowledge.

Our practical, industry-focused approach is designed to help individuals build better careers and organisations create safer, more productive workplaces. We are committed to empowering professionals with the skills, knowledge and globally accepted credentials they need to succeed in today's competitive health, safety and quality sector.
